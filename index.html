Your receipts stay here.

Tesseract’s open neural recognition model reads the image in a browser worker. Photos and recognized text stay in this browser’s local database. There is no inference server or analytics. Hosting still receives normal page and asset requests. Exported files contain your chosen data, so share them deliberately.

This version supports image receipts in BRL. Recognition can make mistakes: approval is always manual. It does not validate tax documents or connect to banks. Clearing the workspace removes the local receipt records. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; download an evidence packet to keep a copy.
